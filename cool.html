Introduction

Cool Realm (or cool world) is a realm made purely for entertaining purposes as a non-related realm for worlds 1 and 2. This world is accessed by a portal which is hidden in a certain place.

Disclaimer: This is unofficial and some information might be incorrect. Also, it is the first time making a guide for GCI so some places might be flawed; apologies for that. If any information is incorrect, please ping @tk601 in the official Discord channel #cool-world.

For more info about the portal location, check the GCI official discord server. The capitalization of some in-game names are lowercase since the realm has a lowercase name of that name.

Important: Do at least 1 or more galactic(s) to unlock this realm/world.

The official GCI development team (including Crow and Spassman) mentioned that this realm might not be permanent, so check for the newest information at the official server.

1. Beginning and Prestige

The first thing you notice is that this realm has hand-written graphics and all words are not capitalized. For the first thing to do is literally the same thing you did when you started the game. Collect grass, buy grass upgrades, rinse and repeat. However, there are few mechanics that have changed before prestige.

1-1. Automation

Same as World 1, automation will be important later on. You want to first grind for grass to unlock the first level of "autocut1".

You should not try to max out the upgrade, but getting 2/5 levels will be better as the recovery from prestige and other resets will be faster later on.

1-2. Perks

In this realm, perks are unlocked at Level 50, which should not be hard to grind for since the "xp1" upgrade doubles every 5 levels instead of the normal 25 levels.

You should also increase the grass cap and regen speed so that it is more efficient as it goes. After you have reached 50 levels, you should unlock Perks. These are useful since they give you massive boosts in grass and xp. Instead of the usual 1 perk when you level up, there are blue grass like platinum and observatorium which you collect and earn perks.

It is preferred to firstly afford the first 2 upgrades, “xpP” and “grassValueP” since they boost their values massively and help you in the run.

After some time, you should be able to reach level 100 to unlock the first reset layer in this realm, prestige.

1-3. Prestige and Recovery

On your first prestige, you should definitely get more than 100 PP (prestige points) since it will help in the upgrades. While you should not grind for too long, definitely keep grinding even when level 100 is reached.

If you can, you want to buy the grass upgrade called “pp1” which boosts prestige points gain.

When you have prestiged for the first time, you will unlock “cooler levels” which is similar to “Tiers” in World 1, but it uses the normal level formula for its required xp. Cooler levels boost perks, which is useful since perks are used to upgrade perk related upgrades.

2. Crystallize

2-1. Prestige and Be Stronger

Each time when you prestige, you should aim for more prestige points so that you can upgrade more and aim for higher. Also, start buying the 2nd tier for the perk upgrades which is called “grassValue2P” and “xp2P” which will multiply its multiplier by another magnitude.

Since prestige reset only utilizes levels, you should keep upgrading level-related upgrades (and also cooler level related upgrades, which is going to be very useful).

Keep grinding until you reach Level 250 when you will unlock your 2nd reset layer, crystallize!

2-2. Crystallize and Recovery

Congrats on reaching Level 250! You can now crystallize. The crystal gain is based on cooler levels (told you to upgrade them so you can get more crystal...).

You should get roughly 100-200 crystals when you reset. You can spend on crystal upgrades. You can buy anything here since every upgrade is powerful, but I will recommend buying for “xp3” and “xp3a” (boosts xp and cooler xp).

3. Grasshop

3-1. More Crystallizing

After your first crystallize, you should keep increasing your levels and cooler level multiplier so you can crystallize faster and get more crystals when you crystallize.

One more important upgrade when crystallizing is “perks”, which increases perk gain. Perk upgrades are more important in the late game, so consider buying more perk-boosting upgrades so that grass, xp, cooler xp, prestige, and crystals are boosted.

After upgrading every upgrade that boosts level, cooler level, and perks, you still have to grind a bit to eventually reach Level 500.

3-2. First Grasshop + Recovery

The grasshop building is at the same place as world 1. There will be a VERY LONG cutscene (it will show every time when you grasshop until grasshop 5. From grasshop 6, it is excluded).

3-3. Second to Fifth Grasshop (Cooler Perks)

Upon reaching GH 2 (short name for grasshop 2), you will unlock cooler perks (just like perks but cooler?). These are earned by collecting cooler perk grass (red colored).

Cooler perks upgrades are very powerful (especially the boosts of levels and grass, which boosts it by +400%) so you might want to grind for these. Also, they have a second upgrade for perks so definitely get that to unlock higher tiers of perk-related upgrades.

4. Steely

4-1. Charger

The cool realm version of steelie is “steely”, which is unlocked by reaching Level 1000. You should do the steely first since it gives more boost. Your first steely unlocks “the factory”.

You need to reset with at least 10 steel because there will be nothing you can upgrade otherwise.

The first thing to buy when you do the steely is the “charger”. This unlocks the DIY charger which you charge by clicking (this cannot be automated).

It is highly recommended to buy the first 4 upgrades the most in the charger, but most importantly the grass and xp.

4-2. Assembler

Assembler is unlocked when you buy the second upgrade in the factory. This unlocks advanced automation throughout the cool realm.

For getting this, it is recommended that you get more than 6 grasshops in order to unlock it easier (especially the 6th grasshop milestone). It is also recommended to buy the charger “steel” upgrade and cooler perk “steel” upgrade.

4-3. Brickhouse

Brickhouse is a replacement of the anti realm in world 1. It is unlockable purchasing with 100 million steel.

It is highly recommended having at least 11 grasshops to unlock this and also utilizing the upgrades from the charger “steel” upgrade and cooler perk “steel” upgrade.

When you enter this place, it will be a maze and have an entirely new currency pinned over every currency. The currency in this place is called bricks, which is an end-game content for the cool realm. There are brick levels that boost charge, and other brick upgrades that overall improves your grinding journey.

Finally, reach grasshop 13… and you're done! You have unlocked everything in this realm!
