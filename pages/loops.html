Intro

Welcome to World 1’s bonus content! This guide will assume that you have read the optional content section of the grasslands guide. If you have not read that yet I highly recommend doing so, as it contains important information on the steps to do before looping.

TLDR for Grasslands Page 19+: you must reach stage 20k before looping as every 10k stages you are rewarded with an additional loop.

Loop

After reaching Stage 1000, the Loop reset (The End) is unlocked at the entrance of the 10th Dungeon (or in the Loop tower which can be accessed through the synthesis island). Looping will reset everything up to that point except your Flower upgrades, Grassland progress and upgrades (you will still need to rebuild the portal to access Grassland), allocate one to all button, Mastery generation and Planet Run progress.

Upon performing a loop you will be given a global multiplier that increases almost everything (some features and currencies stay unaffected however, like singularities, mastery points, cutting/health etc). This multi starts at x1 (by default) and is increased by +1 every loop you perform (i.e if you have 3 loops then your multi will be x(1+3) = x4).

You will need to reach stage 1K in order to loop again. Thus starts the gameplay of looping, which consists mainly of replaying World 1 faster thanks to the multi and the loop upgrades (more on this in the next section).

Note: Make sure you have stage 20K before looping.

Looplets

After looping at Stage 20k, you will have a total multiplier of x4 and you will be awarded 3 Looplets. Looplets are the currency that is used to buy Loop upgrades in the Loop Shop (found in the Loop Tower). These mainly consist of automation upgrades or keeping certain QOL features on loop. I will go over the upgrades you should buy:

  • Loopier Loops: Increases global multi by 1.5x (this is not additive, it multiplies on top of your multi so you would have a x6 boost after buying this).
  • Planetoid II: Keeps the lines, arcs and stardust autobuyers (you get EL for those currencies).
  • Prism: Keep prism constellation upgrade unlocked on loop.
Note: EL means Everlasting which allows you to buy upgrades without spending currencies.

Unlocks

Doing your first Loop will unlock the following:

  • Permanent access to the Loop Tower and Loop upgrades (the teleport can be pinned in the main island).
  • A new boost in the rare flower shop for Loops per Loop (this increases the amount of loops you get, max of 2 a day. You should buy these).
  • Two new stats that track floor 1 loop multi and total loop multi (Total loop multi is the combined loop multi from all loop tower floors, upgrades and milestones that increase the multi).

Recovery

The buffs provided by the first loop are not major enough to be a significant change from regular gameplay. Though you will notice some changes, as you will be able to clear sections significantly faster than before.

I’ll go over the areas that are most affected:

  • Pre-Galactic: you will be able to get AGH 19 on your first galactic to immediately unlock the star accumulator (this is because loop multi decreases the amount of rocket parts required to build the rocket and Anti-realm will be significantly faster.)
  • Galactic: You will be able to get more speed tree upgrades and skip some AGHGS milestones (you still need to do a sacrifice to unlock the recelerator in the funny machine).
  • Planetoid: Achieving AGHGS 45 will be swift (it is also worth noting that the dark tree’s cooldown is also reduced by loop multi). Post ring break will also be much faster thanks to the loop upgrades making constellations and unlocking the star faster (you will still need 1600 trial score).
  • Solarians: Early solarians (stage 1-DM cent) will not be much different aside from the boost to collection resources, Souls/Divine Souls and getting some centralizes earlier. After unlocking grassland and getting sharper edge you’ll be able to effortlessly fly through stages and skip many twilight stages (you’ll also immediately have access to synthesis at stage 140 with your upgraded synthesis gem).

Checklist

Here are a list of things that you might have forgotten since the first playthrough:

  • You can right-click on upgrades to buy max in case you’re playing this game on pc.
  • You can use Autobuy Adv. to change how much an autobuyer will use up a currency for each upgrade.
  • A good AGH milestone route to do at the start would be AGH19, AGH13, AGH7, then AGHGS 3. Don’t forget to do star runs when you hit a wall.
  • Don’t forget about the solar rays generator unlocked at star tier 5, you can find it in the advanced tab.
  • Portal Piece 10 requires a grassland gem, which can be obtained after centralizing Dark matter. If you did a Twilight, then you need to go to synthesis and get an empty gem.
  • Dark charge centralize requires you to have a last prism and a T12 grid constellation on your setup.

2nd Loop

After reaching stage 1K, you are once again given the option to loop. Reach stage 20K first then use 4 Loop per Loop boosts from the rare flower shop, this will increase the loops you’ll gain to 7 allowing you to reach 10 total loops. Performing the loop will open a new door, going through it grants you access to floor 2.

DO NOT GET MORE THAN 10 TOTAL LOOPS, this is because all your floor 1 progress will be reset on floor 2.

Mega Loop

Good job on reaching the loop tower's second floor. Floor 2 is a big step up from the first one in terms of length and difficulty.

First and foremost you will have to perform your first Mega Loop (ML). The Mega Loop reset will reset everything Loop does as well as Loops, Looplets, floor 1 Loop multi and Looplet upgrades and gives a base global multi of x10. (it is worth noting that each loop floor’s multi is multiplicative with other floors and additive with itself, for example having 5 loops and 2 mega loop gives you a global multi of: x6 x20 = x120)

Make sure to perform your first mega loop immediately when you can, as getting more loops is a waste.

Note: There is no way to get more than 1 mega loop on your first mega loop.

Mega Looplets

Performing your first mega loop will award you with 1 Mega Looplet, this can be used to buy powerful upgrades.

The most important upgrade is the Mega Loop per Mega Loop upgrade costing 10 Looplets. It allows you to get two mega loops at once. This upgrade is crucial to progression and thus you should always buy it before performing a mega loop. Do not buy regular looplet upgrades past this point.

As for Mega Looplet upgrades I’ll go over the order you should buy them in:

  • (ML 1) Mega Loopier Loops: this gives you a x1.4 multi buff on top of the x1.5 from the previous upgrade (Loopier loops) which is a x2.1 buff in total. (Some loop upgrades will state that they will give you the previous one, make sure you read the upgrade’s descriptions carefully)
  • (ML 3) Mega Planetoid I OR II and Pre Galactic Realms: buying Planetoid I or II is up to preference whether you want multi planetary and automations, or lines, arcs, stardust EL and automation. Pre galactic realms gives you normal realm and anti-realm automation including multi grasshop and multi grass skip.
  • (ML 5) Mega Planetoid I OR II (the one you didn’t buy yet) and Galactic realms: Galactic realms gives you Unnatural realm automation and multi grass jump and star/moonstone/dark matter automation (NO EL).
  • (ML7 to ML11): Do not spend Mega Looplets at this point, save them for floor 3 (more on this later).

ML Unlocks

Performing your first Mega Loop will unlock the following:

  • The Lime charger (more on this in the next section).
  • The 11th Dungeon Neon City (more on this in the next sections).
  • New Tab in the Loop Shop (more on this in the next sections).
  • New Mastery upgrades.
  • Permanently unlocks grassland (no need for the portal you can teleport to it in the main island).
  • Additional Combat Ranks in Grasslands.

Lime Charge

The Lime Charger is a new feature unlocked at Mega Loop. It works identically to the normal and dark chargers. It generates a base of 1 lime charge (LC) per second and has 3 unique milestones that will help you throughout your loop journey. The most important is milestone 3, which is shown below.

  • Milestone 3 (requires 1Mil (1e6) lime charge): this gives you +1 loop per loop for every 6 Orders of Magnitude (OoMs) of Lime Charge you have at a time.

Lime Charge is increased by Loop multi and Dungeon 11 gear (more on this next section). You can also spend LC in the new Tab of the Loop Shop. This tab will have upgrades that give you EL for some currencies.

Note: Mega Looping will reset your current LC, so spend any LC you have on the upgrades in the EL tab before doing a Mega Loop as those upgrades are not reset until later on.

Dungeon 11

Gear Required for D11’s First Room: Mythics from DD4.

D11 is the as of right now the final unlockable grassland dungeon. This dungeon consists of a single level with a lot more rooms than regular dungeons which ramp up in difficulty as you go.

The first room in D11 is the only room that makes use of normal cutting/health. You will need around 10Sx cutting to beat the first enemies. Killing them will drop Neon gear, you will notice that their stats are cyan and that they are way smaller than your current gear, which might lead you to believe that they are worse.

This isn't true because the stats are indicating a new type of cutting “Advanced Cutting/Health” where 1 Advanced cutting is 1Sp Normal Cutting. You should immediately equip the commons else you will not be able to progress.

You will also notice that the armor/weapons will provide you with a base LC boost instead of Collection/Forming/Restoration, those boosts are instead provided by the Synthesis gem found in this dungeon.

D11’s progression is very different from other dungeons as you’ll need to increase your loop multi in order to generate more gold/shards and upgrade/ascend your gear to make it further. You should try to get the uncommon gear in early-mid Mega Loop and the rare gear in Later Mega Loops. This is crucial to get the 1m LC necessary for the + 1lpl boost. (You shouldn't go for the legendary as it requires a lot of grinding for minor benefits).

You will also notice the new mastery upgrades. These upgrades are expensive on top of having extremely harsh scaling. You will need to master D11 items as they increase mastery generation the most. While spending Mastery Points you should prioritize Advanced Combat Power and Sell/scrap value upgrades. At this point you will also be able to increase your combat rank several times.

ML Recovery

At Mega Loop you will be able to skip far more things, I’ll go over the changes you can apply to your loop runs:

  • Early game + Galactic: you will be able to skip more aghgs milestones and completely bypass waiting for steel and rocket parts.
  • Planetoid: almost all time walls are gone at this point, the wait for aghgs45 is nonexistent, dark fruits are given at a much faster rate, you can afford to make hybrid constellation setups (ones that buff every currency), grass jump 35 is easier to achieve and you no longer have to wait for the star.
  • Supernova: you can skip star tier 1 entirely and go immediately to star tier 2, you will also need fewer supernovas to reach the eclipse requirements.
  • Solarians: your grasslands gear will give cutting edge an even bigger effect and your better synthesis gem will also help a lot more. You can do your first Twilight after SNT 11.

The most effective way to achieve Mega Loops at this point is to reach stage 20K and use 2 lpl boosts loop then reach stage 20k again for 10 total loops. Alternatively for more passive players they can reach stage 20k and wait for 6 lpl boosts.

( TWO stage 20Ks = 6 loops + 1 loop from LC + 2 loops from lpl) = 10 loops

( stage 20k = 3 + 1 loop from LC + 6 lpl ) = 10 loops

Note: It is not worth waiting for 1T (1e12) lime charge as it is too slow.

ML 10

At this point you should have 6 Mega Looplets saved (if you hadn’t bought upgrades past ML5), you will be able to go through the cyan door and access the 3rd floor.

Your Mega Looplets will be converted into a new currency at a rate of 1:10 (1 Mega Looplet = 10 of said currency) once you perform the Super Mega Loop reset.

Note: It is not worth going for more mega loops after ml11 to farm for the currency, it is a waste of time.
