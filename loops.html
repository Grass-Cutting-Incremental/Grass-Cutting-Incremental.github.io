Introduction & Notice

Welcome to World 1’s bonus content! This guide will assume that you have read the optional content section of the grasslands guide.

TLDR for Grasslands Page 19+: You must reach stage 20k before looping as every 10k stages you are rewarded with an additional loop.

Loop Floor 1: Loop

After reaching Stage 1000, the Loop reset (The End) is unlocked. Looping will reset everything up to that point except your Flower upgrades, Grassland progress and upgrades.

Upon performing a loop you will be given a global multiplier that increases almost everything. This multi starts at x1 and is increased by +1 every loop you perform.

Make sure you have stage 20K before looping.

Looplets and Looplet upgrades

After looping at Stage 20k, you will have a total multiplier of x4 and you will be awarded 3 Looplets. Looplets are the currency that is used to buy Loop upgrades.

Recommended Upgrades:

  • Loopier Loops: Increases global multi by 1.5x.
  • Planetoid II: Keeps the lines, arcs and stardust autobuyers.
  • Prism: Keep prism constellation upgrade unlocked on loop.

Loop Unlocks

Doing your first Loop will unlock the following:

  • Permanent access to the Loop Tower and Loop upgrades.
  • A new boost in the rare flower shop for Loops per Loop (You should buy these).
  • Two new stats that track floor 1 loop multi and total loop multi.

Loop Recovery

The buffs provided by the first loop are not major enough to be a significant change from regular gameplay, but you will be able to clear sections faster.

  • Pre-Galactic: Get AGH 19 on your first galactic to unlock the star accumulator immediately.
  • Galactic: Get more speed tree upgrades and skip some AGHGS milestones.
  • Planetoid: Achieving AGHGS 45 will be swift.
  • Solarians: After unlocking grasslands and getting sharper edge you’ll be able to effortlessly fly through stages.

Things You May Have Forgotten

  • You can right-click on upgrades to buy max.
  • Portal Piece 10 requires a grasslands gem (centralize Dark Matter).
  • Dark charge centralize requires you to have a last prism and a T12 grid constellation.

Second Loop (IMPORTANT)

After reaching stage 1K, you are once again given the option to loop.

DO NOT GET MORE THAN 10 TOTAL LOOPS, this is because all your floor 1 progress will be reset on floor 2.

Reach stage 20K first then use 4 Loop per Loop boosts from the rare flower shop. This will increase the loops you’ll gain to 7 allowing you to reach 10 total loops.

Loop Floor 2: Mega Loop

Floor 2 is a big step up from the first one. First and foremost you will have to perform your first Mega Loop (ML). The Mega Loop reset will reset everything Loop does as well as Loops, Looplets, and Looplet upgrades.

Make sure to perform your first mega loop immediately when you can, as getting more loops is a waste.

Mega Looplets and Upgrades

Performing your first mega loop will award you with 1 Mega Looplet. The most important upgrade is the Mega Loop per Mega Loop upgrade costing 10 Looplets. It allows you to get two mega loops at once.

Order to buy:

  • (ML 1) Mega Loopier Loops
  • (ML 3) Mega Planetoid I OR II and Pre Galactic Realms
  • (ML 5) Mega Planetoid I OR II (the one you didn’t buy yet) and Galactic realms
  • (ML7 to ML11) Do not spend Mega Looplets at this point, save them for floor 3.

Mega Loop Unlocks

Performing your first Mega Loop will unlock the Lime charger, the 11th Dungeon, New Mastery upgrades, and permanently unlocks grassland.

The Lime Charger + LC Tab

The Lime Charger generates a base of 1 lime charge (LC) per second. The most important is milestone 3 (1M LC) which gives you +1 loop per loop for every 6 OoMs of Lime Charge.

Mega Looping will reset your current LC, so spend any LC you have on the upgrades in the EL tab before doing a Mega Loop.

Dungeon 11: Neon City

Gear Required: Mythics from DD4.

The first room in D11 is the only room that makes use of normal cutting/health. Killing them will drop Neon gear. Their stats indicate a new type of cutting Advanced Cutting/Health where 1 Advanced cutting is 1Sp Normal Cutting.

You should try to get the uncommon gear in early-mid Mega Loop and the rare gear in Later Mega Loops. This is crucial to get the 1m LC necessary for the +1lpl boost.

Mega Loop Recovery

At Mega Loop you will be able to skip far more things.

  • Early/Galactic: Bypass waiting for steel and rocket parts.
  • Planetoid: Almost all time walls are gone. Dark fruits are faster.
  • Supernova: Skip star tier 1 entirely and go immediately to star tier 2.
  • Solarians: You can do your first Twilight after SNT 11.

Mega Loop 11 (Final)

At this point you should have 6 Mega Looplets saved. You will be able to go through the cyan door and access the 3rd floor.

Your Mega Looplets will be converted into a new currency at a rate of 1:10 once you perform the Super Mega Loop reset.

It is not worth going for more mega loops after ml11.
