Introduction & Notice

You have reached Solarians! This is the beginning of the long grind towards your next major goal. Before you get into the guide, there is some important and useful information to note below.

You should avoid any of the following actions:

  • Spending any Flowers in Flower Shop II.
  • Forgetting to purchase upgrades in all tabs of the Singularity chart (accessible via slider).
  • Performing a Sunrise for a lower amount of Sunrise FM.

Guide Info & Formatting

This guide will use abbreviations, with the first use of them being marked in bold next to the full version i.e. Fighting Multi (FM). Names of resets and currencies will also be marked in bold for clarity.

Basic Tutorial

Solarians is a minigame involving time and material management. You will be using the people known as Solarians to collect materials and form buffs for you to progress. These Solarians have speeds they work at, with Collection Speed for materials and Forming Speed for forming.

You can select the number of Solarians you want to use at one time via the large board to the left of the collection. I would personally recommend only using the 100, 25, 5 or 1 options at any given time.

The only way to progress and eventually beat this minigame is to increase your stage by fighting enemies. Increasing your stage will provide you with temporary buffs. The main boosts to pay attention to here are Solar Shards and Sunrise Fighting Multi (SFM).

SFM does not apply immediately, requiring a Sunrise to be performed. I personally recommend getting between 5x and 100x SFM each Sunrise.

IMPORTANT: Solar Rays Generation is an upgrade unlocked in the Solar Upgrades Board at Stage 3, costing 10 Million Solar Shards. It should be purchased as soon as possible.

Solarians Unlock

Solarians are unlocked via a board that appears at Supernova Tier 5 (SNT5) called the Singularity Chart. This board is next to the Solar Obelisk. You can obtain Singularities by Centralizing currencies. Get enough Prestige Points for the Prestige Point Centralize to get the Solarians upgrade.

Stage 1 & Souls

By defeating your first enemy you have gained a new currency called Souls. It is recommended to prioritize Solarian Power earlier on since it offers much better boosts than upgrading collecting/forming individually.

You will quickly get the second centralize and have to choose between Remnants and Soul Eater. Choose Remnants because it unlocks 2 upgrades costing Remnants in the Remnants upgrade board.

Sol Compression

Stage 10 is of great significance. It unlocks a new board known as Sol Compression, which generates Compressed Sol. Sol Compression is similar to Dark Fruits but is tiered, and is spent into all available boosts of that tier automatically.

Be sure to purchase the new sunstone upgrade that unlocks at stage 12.

Tier 1 Challenges

With this stage, you have unlocked challenges which will grant challenge score and a currency called Golden Stars. You should do these after you get to SNT6 as this unlocks Bulk Completion for T1 Challenges.

Recommended Spending Order for Golden Stars:

  • First 5: Golden Souls
  • Next 5: Sword Constellation Cap
  • Next 5: Soul Constellation Cap
  • Next 5: Golden Souls
  • Next 10: Forming
  • Next 10: Collecting
  • Last 10: Restore

Restoration Board

The Restoration Board is unlocked at Stage 20. This is how you get beyond SNT 5 and use the new currency known as Mana.

  • Sword vs Soul: Choose Sword first for the fighting boost.
  • Divine Robbery vs Super Compression: Choose Super Compression.

Stage 40

Now, you will need to return to the restoration board where you should have met all the requirements necessary to obtain Supernova Tier 6. If you need Mana, you will need to Time Warp, go AFK for a bit of time, or waste time playing Planet Run.

Supernova Tier 6

Upon reaching the requirements for the next star tier, you are able to increase your SNT. As seen on the restoration board, you now have an Offense/Defense boost that increases based on your star growth, and the first upgrade of the restore tab will now be unlocked.

Other notable unlocks include the new Sunset reset in the Solar Obelisk and new remnant upgrades. If you get Stars and Fun centralize before Sunset, choose Best Offense over Super Absorb.

Sunset

READ THE FULL SECTION BELOW BEFORE DOING A SUNSET

Sunset is the second reset on the Solar Obelisk. It is an important reset, BUT YOU SHOULD NOT DO IT IMMEDIATELY. You will also gain Darkness on this reset at a rate of 1:1 of current Mana.

It is recommended to do your first Sunset reset after obtaining SNT7, and both Stars and Fun centralizes, at around Stage 56-58. This should be for around 400+ Divine Souls and 1 Billion+ Darkness.

Spending Guide:

  • Singularity Chart (Souls Tab): Purchase all upgrades costing 3-5 Divine Souls, Compression II, Light Logs V, Souls II.
  • Singularity Chart (Mana Tab): Purchase ‘Violent’ upgrade.
  • Souls Board: Buy a mix of Compression, FM, Mana and Souls. Ignore Restoration Speed for now.
  • Darkness: Prioritize a new Grid tier over anything else.

Tier 2 Challenges

After performing a Sunset, you have unlocked T2 Challenges. They provide a second type of stars known as Platinum Stars. Completions in the T2 challenge called ‘Challenging’ also automatically complete T1 challenges you may have missed.

It is not recommended to skip T2 challenges.

Supernova Tier 7

Upon reaching the requirements for SNT7 you can increase your supernova tier. This tier is notable for its benefits which include:

  • A new star growth buff for restoration speed.
  • A new tab for the restoration board called the portal tab.
  • The Sunset reset no longer resets basic levels in the forming tab (except compression).

Portal Pieces

By reaching SNT7, you have unlocked the Portal tab. You can now craft Portal Pieces (PPs). Every portal piece will require a set amount of resources and restoration to complete.

You will notice that portal pieces 8 and 9 require a huge amount of fragments. The best way to improve your gain is to:

  • Purchase at least two upgrades of grass essence in the souls tab.
  • Get the planet run grass essence upgrade.
  • Use time warps (and don’t sunrise until you build those pieces).

Sunset - Stage 100 Help

Make sure you are centralizing whenever possible and doing sunsets every time you get x5-x20 more Dsouls and darkness. Repeat this process until you reach stage 100.

Doing planet run while waiting is very beneficial especially while waiting for portal pieces.

Portal Piece 10

Once you reach stage 107-110 you should be able to finally centralize Dark Matter. Doing so will teleport you to a small obby, clearing it and doing a Galactic will reward you with the Grassland Gem, which is needed to complete the 10th portal piece. Go and finish the portal to enter the new realm.
